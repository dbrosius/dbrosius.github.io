The ladder

Type a starting word and a target word (same length, 3–6 letters), then hit Solve. The target must be a real English word; the start can be anything, and every rung after it changes exactly one letter.

How it works
  • A bidirectional breadth-first search grows outwards from the target word and from the start at the same time, through the built-in dictionary (one letter changed at a time), and stops the moment the two meet.
  • Only the target has to be a real word: the starting letters may be nonsense, and the first rung it turns into is.
  • Word lengths are limited to 3–6 letters, where word ladders are actually interesting.
  • The chain is always as short as possible; when several chains are equally short, the one built from the most familiar words is chosen.
  • The dictionary is bundled with the page, so the whole puzzle stays on your device.
